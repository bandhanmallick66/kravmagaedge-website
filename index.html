About Krav Maga Edge

A modern self-defense academy in Hyderabad, training individuals, communities and companies in person and online.

What we are

A modern self-defense academy

Krav Maga Edge is a modern self-defense academy built to develop knowledge, the right mindset, capability, confidence and readiness for the real world.

Why we exist

Beyond learning techniques

Our training goes beyond learning techniques. We develop the awareness to recognize danger, the composure to make decisions under pressure, and the practical skills to respond when it matters most.

Vision

Forging a self-defense mindset

Forging a self-defense mindset in every individual, through the art of Krav Maga.

Mission

An essential life skill

To make Krav Maga self-defense an essential life skill by integrating four components of training.

  1. 01Physical fitness
  2. 02Skill development
  3. 03Mental training
  4. 04Cognitive growth
